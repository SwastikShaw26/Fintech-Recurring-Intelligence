# Customer validation prep

Three interviews are planned. Responses and market validation are pending. Recruit one India-based consumer with repeat UPI/card payments, one independent freelancer with mixed expenses, and one solo proprietor or independent consultant managing renewals. Ask permission before taking notes or recording. Use synthetic demo data and avoid collecting statements, account numbers, or credentials.

## Interview form

- Participant ID, interview date, role, location at city or region level, and preferred language.
- Consent for anonymized notes and, separately, for recording or a de-identified quote.
- Tell me about the last time you reconciled recurring payments. What triggered it, and what tools did you use?
- Which payments were hardest to recognize, classify, forecast, or cancel? What did you do next?
- Walk through one recent missed renewal or unclear charge. What time or cost did it create?
- With the synthetic example, find one recurring payment, explain why it was detected, and identify what is still uncertain.
- For a work expense, show how you would separate it and assess its expected monthly cost. What would make you trust the estimate?
- Compare the English and Hindi explanations if relevant. Which would you use, and what financial context would you consent to sharing?
- What would prevent you from importing a statement? What would make this worth using again over your current workflow?

## Market Fit Canvas

| Area | Initial view to test |
| --- | --- |
| Target customer | Indian consumers and independent workers tracking recurring payments across statements |
| Job to be done | Explain repeat debits, anticipate upcoming charges, and separate business costs |
| Current alternatives | Bank or UPI apps, spreadsheets, calendar reminders, manual accountant review |
| Friction | Unfamiliar descriptors, incomplete statements, and disconnected payment tools |
| Proposed value | Inspectable evidence and qualified estimates with optional bilingual explanation |
| Initial channel | Freelancer communities and personal-finance groups |
| Fit signals | Independently completes tasks, accurately describes uncertainty, and identifies a concrete reason to return |
| Open questions | Import trust, willingness to maintain labels, expected refresh cadence, and willingness to pay |

## Persona Use Case Matrix

| Persona | Trigger | Task | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UPI/card consumer | Suspected repeat debit | Find a recurrence and its source records | Whether they can explain the inference and choose a sensible next action |
| Freelancer | Monthly bookkeeping | Include work expenses and read the recurring estimate | Whether the temporary planner is useful and which saved-label behavior is required |
| Solo proprietor | Upcoming renewal or cash planning | Interpret forecast freshness and incomplete coverage | Whether they correctly distinguish a projection from a confirmed debit |

## Initial hypotheses

1. Two of three participants can explain a detected recurrence using evidence without prompting.
2. At least one freelancer or proprietor identifies a real repeat workflow that durable labels would improve.
3. Two of three participants correctly interpret the stale-history and coverage warnings.
4. A Hindi-preferring participant finds the Hindi option helpful and understands the consent boundary. If no participant prefers Hindi, leave this hypothesis untested.

These are pilot criteria for three interviews. Record contradictory evidence and avoid extrapolating to the market from this sample.
