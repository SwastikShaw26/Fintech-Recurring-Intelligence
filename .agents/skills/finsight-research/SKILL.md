# FinSight Technical Research Skill

Use this skill when investigating technical problems, algorithms, standards, and Indian regulatory compliance for FinSight.

## Instructions
1. Formulate specific, falsifiable research questions.
2. Search authoritative sources (peer-reviewed literature, RBI circulars, NPCI frameworks).
3. Do not fabricate citations or URLs. Include exact circular numbers and publication years.
4. Compare technical trade-offs across algorithmic alternatives (heuristics vs FFT/Autocorrelation vs DBSCAN/HDBSCAN).
5. Output structured findings to `docs/engineering-evaluation/research-report.md`.
