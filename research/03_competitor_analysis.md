# Competitor Analysis

Research date: 2026-10-27
Purpose: Identify existing capabilities and evidence-backed opportunities.
Note: Product pages describe vendor claims; they are not independent
performance evaluations.

## 1. Rocket Money

Official recurring-payment help:
https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/2185531-managing-bills-and-subscriptions

Official cancellation help:
https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/934402-how-do-i-cancel-a-subscription-on-rocket-money

Observed capabilities from official help pages:
- Analyses linked financial transactions to identify recurring bills.
- Shows recurring payments and upcoming items.
- Allows manual additions and corrections.
- Supports cancellation requests for some services.
- Provides self-cancellation instructions when direct assistance is unavailable.

Relevance:
Recurring detection and cancellation assistance already exist as a product
category. We must not claim those features alone are unique.

## 2. Paylert

Google Play listing:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nishkamaapps.paylert

The listing advertises subscription tracking and detection using SMS,
including UPI AutoPay and bank-mandate related payments.

Relevance:
There are India-focused products targeting hidden recurring charges.
Our comparison should focus on tested functionality, transparency, and
evaluation results rather than assumptions about competitor weaknesses.

## 4. Proposed differentiation

1. Publish a reproducible benchmark on labelled synthetic data.
2. Report annual and irregular detection separately.
3. Explain every detection using dates, amounts and merchant evidence.
4. Separate observed facts from inferred subscriptions and mandates.
5. Provide cancellation guidance for Indian payment mechanisms.
6. Include uncertainty and abstain from strong claims when evidence is weak.

## 5. Fair comparison policy

- Do not claim that no competitor supports a feature without verification.
- Do not compare our synthetic accuracy directly to vendor marketing claims.
- Recheck product pages before the final presentation.
- Record unsupported or unknown entries as unknown, not as missing features.
